Dare Digital Repository

Dare is an AI-enhanced, open-access institutional repository that preserves, organises and intelligently searches theses, research papers, datasets and institutional archives — freely discoverable to the world at dspace.dare.co.zw.

For researchers & staff

Have work to share? Sign in to Dare.

Reading is open to everyone. A free account unlocks the depositing side of the repository — put your research where the world can find, cite and build on it.

  • Deposit theses, papers, datasets and reports in minutes
  • Get a permanent, citable link (persistent identifier) for every item
  • Track views and downloads on your work over time
  • Manage your collection and update metadata whenever you need
  • AI-assisted metadata and subject tagging as you submit
