  • Get a permanent, citable link (persistent identifier) for every item
  • Track views and downloads on your work over time

FAQ

Dare — frequently asked questions

Is Dare free to use?

Yes. Searching, browsing, reading and downloading are completely free and need no account.

Do I need an account?

Only to deposit or manage work. Reading and downloading open items is open to everyone, anonymously.

Who can deposit research?

Researchers, students and staff of participating institutions. Depositing rights are set by your institution.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes — a free Android app at repo.dare.co.zw lets you search, read and download research on your phone.

What can I find in Dare?

Theses and dissertations, research papers, datasets, reports and institutional archives — across every collection.
