Grounded by design

Your documents in. Cited answers out.

ChengetAi AI connects directly to the systems we already host for you — DSpace, Koha, OJS, Nextcloud, file archives — indexes them securely, and answers questions with citations back to the source.

  • Connects to repositories, catalogues and document stores
  • Answers are grounded — every claim links to a source
  • Runs on ChengetAi Cloud: your data never leaves your tenancy
  • Works in English and African languages
