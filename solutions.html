👤 "Mbeu yangu yechibage yava nehunyoro hwakawanda. Ndoitei?" (My maize is too moist. What should I do?)

🤖 Target ≤13.5% moisture before bagging. Sun-dry on tarpaulins for 2–3 days, stir every 2 hours, and do the salt-bottle test before storage. Hermetic bags will then protect against weevils without chemicals.
