About ChengetAi Labs

A technology platform company, made in Africa

"Chengeta" means to keep, to protect, to preserve. ChengetAi Labs exists to keep Africa's knowledge safe, its institutions online and its future intelligent.

Vision & Mission

Why we exist

Our vision — to become Africa's leading provider of intelligent digital infrastructure, enabling organisations to innovate, preserve knowledge and deliver world-class digital services.

To design, deploy and support AI-powered digital platforms that improve education, research, agriculture, enterprise productivity and public service delivery — while fostering innovation across Africa.

ChengetAi Labs is a Zimbabwean technology company — not just a software vendor. We provide the complete digital infrastructure lifecycle — cloud hosting, deployment automation and AI-powered management — combining AI with open-source technologies and cloud-native infrastructure to make advanced technology practical, affordable and accessible.
