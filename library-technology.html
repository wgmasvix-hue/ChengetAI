Integrated Library System (ILS)

📚 Koha — the world's leading open-source library management system

Run your entire library on Koha: cataloguing (MARC21 / RDA), circulation, acquisitions, serials, the OPAC and reports. We tune Zebra or Elasticsearch search, configure SIP2 for self-checkout, and connect RFID and barcode hardware.

  • New Koha installation, upgrades and version migration
  • Data migration from Liberty, Alexandria, SLIMS, spreadsheets or legacy ILS
  • Z39.50 / SRU copy cataloguing and union catalogues
  • OPAC theming, SIP2 self-service and hosting on ChengetAI Cloud

Institutional Repository

🗄 DSpace — institutional & digital repositories

Preserve and share theses, dissertations, research output and archives with DSpace — the most widely used repository platform in the world. Open-access ready with OAI-PMH harvesting, persistent identifiers and Solr discovery.

  • DSpace 7 (Angular UI) installs, upgrades and migrations
  • OAI-PMH, handle/DOI identifiers and Google Scholar indexing
  • Bulk import (SAF), metadata mapping and IR branding
  • AI-enhanced semantic search — see our live Dare repository
