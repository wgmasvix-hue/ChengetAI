SkillMatch

AI-powered skills matching that connects African talent with opportunity. SkillMatch reads what people can actually do — qualifications, experience, portfolios — and matches them to jobs, gigs, apprenticeships and training that fit.

  • Candidates — build a skills profile and get matched, not just listed
  • Employers — describe the work; AI surfaces the people who can do it
  • Colleges & polytechnics — track graduate skills into employment
  • Powered by ChengetAi AI matching on ChengetAi Cloud
Get Early Access For Employers

How matching works

  • Skills are extracted from CVs, certificates and portfolios
  • Roles are broken down into the skills they really need
  • AI ranks fit both ways — person to role, role to person
  • Gaps become recommendations for training on ChengetAi Learn
