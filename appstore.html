SkillMatch

AI-powered skills matching that connects African talent with opportunity. SkillMatch reads what people can actually do — qualifications, experience, portfolios — and matches them to jobs, gigs, apprenticeships and training that fit.

  • Candidates — build a skills profile and get matched, not just listed
  • Employers — describe the work; AI surfaces the people who can do it
  • Colleges & polytechnics — track graduate skills into employment
  • Powered by ChengetAi AI matching on ChengetAi Cloud
Get Early Access For Employers

How matching works

  • Skills are extracted from CVs, certificates and portfolios
  • Roles are broken down into the skills they really need
  • AI ranks fit both ways — person to role, role to person
  • Gaps become recommendations for training on ChengetAi Learn

For Developers

Publish your app on the ChengetAi Store

Built an Android app for African users? Submit your APK — we review, scan and host it, and your app reaches institutions and users across the continent.

✅
ℹ️ The submissions service isn't reachable right now. Please use the contact form with a link to your APK, and our team will process it manually.

How review works

  • Submit your APK with a description and category
  • Automated malware and permissions scan
  • Human review against store policies
  • Approved apps are listed with your developer name
  • Updates go through the same pipeline — versioned, always

What we look for

  • Works offline or on low bandwidth where possible
  • No hidden data collection — permissions must match purpose
  • Clear value for African users and institutions
