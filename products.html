Knowledge Preservation

🗂 Dare Digital Repository

An AI-enhanced institutional repository platform designed to preserve, organise and intelligently search academic and research content — repository management meets modern AI-powered knowledge discovery.

  • Preservation-grade storage for theses, papers and archives
  • AI-powered semantic search across full collections
  • Automated metadata enrichment and classification
  • Open standards — OAI-PMH, persistent identifiers, indexing
  • Runs standalone or alongside your existing DSpace
Request a Dare Demo

Why Dare is different

  • Ask questions in plain language — answers cite the collection
  • Discovery works in English and African languages
  • AI suggests metadata so librarians curate, not retype
  • Built and supported in Africa, on ChengetAi Cloud
