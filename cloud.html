Capabilities

Everything a modern institution needs to operate

🖥

VPS Hosting

Flexible virtual servers from 1 vCPU to high-memory instances, provisioned in minutes.

🛠

Managed Servers

Our engineers patch, tune and secure your servers so your team can focus on outcomes.

💾

Backups

Automated, encrypted backups with scheduled restore drills — because a backup is only real once it's been restored.

📈

Monitoring

Prometheus and Grafana dashboards with alerting on CPU, RAM, storage, network and services.

🔐

SSL

Free TLS certificates on every service, issued and renewed automatically.

Security

Hardened images, firewalls, fail2ban, intrusion detection and security patching by default.

Docker-Native

Launch a server

Choose size, region and OS. Your server boots with monitoring, SSL and security pre-wired.

Deploy software
