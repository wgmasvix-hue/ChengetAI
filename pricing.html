Questions

Pricing FAQ

Can we start small and upgrade later?
Yes — every plan upgrades in place. Your servers, deployments and data move with you; upgrading never requires a migration.
What does "fully managed" include?
On Managed Cloud we handle OS patching, application upgrades, security hardening, backup verification, monitoring and incident response. You get a monthly report and a human to call.
Do you offer education or public-sector discounts?
Yes. Accredited schools, colleges, universities, public libraries and government agencies qualify for sector pricing — ask sales for the current schedule.
Where is our data hosted?
ChengetAi Cloud runs on vetted infrastructure partners with regions relevant to African institutions, and disaster-recovery replicas in a second region. Enterprise plans can align hosting to data-residency requirements.
Is there a free trial?
We offer a 14-day evaluation for Starter and Professional, and a guided proof-of-concept for Enterprise — book a demo to arrange one.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your institution and we'll recommend the right starting point — no obligation.
