Questions

Pricing FAQ

How is the platform priced?
Enterprise pricing is tailored to your users, modules, deployment model and support needs. Request a quote and we'll scope it precisely — no surprises.
Can we deploy on our own infrastructure?
Yes. Enterprise and Government plans support on-premises, private and hybrid deployment, including air-gapped environments. Your data never leaves your tenancy.
Do you offer public-sector or education pricing?
Yes — accredited institutions and government agencies qualify for sector pricing. Ask sales for the current schedule.
How long does implementation take?
Most deployments reach production in weeks, not years, using ChengetAI Deploy. We provide onboarding and a named engineer on Enterprise and Government plans.
Can we start with a pilot?
Absolutely. We run guided proofs-of-concept on a pilot corpus so you see grounded results on your own data before committing. Book a demo to arrange one.
